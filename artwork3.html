rose garden

Tianyi Evans Gu (b. 2006), 2025
Motors, newspaper clippings, cardboard

it's pretty, is it not? the middle is left empty—that one is for you to fill.

Try it yourself (10 min):

Retitle the sculpture and fold your own origami flower to fill the center. This is your blank slate. How about some color! Let yourself be guided by your muse ("impetus for creation"). Go wild.

Leave your completed flower (if you wish, you can also take it) right by the sculpture. This is an offering to the garden.
