Marionette in Motion

TIANYI EVANS GU (B. 2006) • 2025

An exhibition on potential.

motion moves.

it catches the eye with Flourish and Blur.

it feels like Breath flying away.

French: marionnette → "little Mary"

Greek: puppet → νευρόσπαστος → "drawn by strings"

"fulfillment, realization" ← actuality ←→ potentiality → "it can be, possibility"

when motion doesn't move,

i am left with

Breath in Lungs—trapped.

i am caught by the

Reality and Stillness.

but motion left Stagnant is coming.

it is "dunamis"—Power.

it is Possibility.

are you Ready?

Marionette in Motion is about unfulfilled realization. It is ready for motion, but not quite yet. It is crumpled newspaper clippings and paperclips and shoelaces. But it is motors and wires and electronic boards too. It holds the tension between what is and what could be. Can you feel it?

clothesline

I grew up with a clothesline in my backyard. On it hung clothes and blankets and memories too...

rose garden

Motors, newspaper clippings, cardboard

it's pretty, is it not? the middle is left empty—that one is for you to fill.
